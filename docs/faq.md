# FAQ

## Are the first 10 conversions really free?

Yes. Copy and download are included, and no card is required.

## What happens after 10 conversions?

The next conversion requires a paid bundle from the [pricing page](https://markitdown.store/pricing/).

## Do paid bundles renew automatically?

No. The displayed monthly and annual packages are one-time payments.

## Is this an official Microsoft service?

No. It is an independent hosted companion.
